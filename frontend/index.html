    Account

    Your username and password are how you get back in. Your display name, picture, and the line about you are what everyone else sees.

    Display name

    Username

    Role

    Password & security

    Changing your password signs you out on every device.

    Voice

    Calls use one noise suppressor, echo cancellation, and 64 kbps speech. Picture quality and frame rate apply to your camera and to anything you share.

    Microphone

    The input used when you join voice.

    Noise suppression

    Automatic uses the system voice isolation when this PC has it, and Waystone’s noise model otherwise.

    Echo cancellation

    Keeps the room from hearing itself through your speakers.

    Automatic gain

    Evens out a quiet or loud microphone.

    Picture quality

    720, 1080, or HQ. HQ is 1440. A camera that is already on updates immediately. A share uses this the next time it restarts.

    Frame rate

    How often the picture updates, separate from its size. 30 is the usual choice. 60 is the heavy one.

    Expressions

    These rules belong to the room. They apply to everyone, and they stay on the server. Picture quality, the microphone, and the tray stay on each person’s own device.

    Who can add emoji and stickers

    Existing pictures stay either way. This only decides who can upload a new one.

    Emoji size

    From 16 KB to 512 KB. Emoji appear inside a message as :name:.

    Sticker size

    From 32 KB to 2 MB. A sticker is sent as its own picture.

    How many of each

    The shelf holds this many emoji, and the same number of stickers.

    System

    Waystone can stay available without sitting in the taskbar.

    Close to the tray

    The close button hides Waystone beside the clock. Voice stays connected. Quit from the tray icon when you want to leave, which is also when a downloaded update installs.

    Start with Windows

    Open Waystone when you sign in to this PC.

    The installed app uses this switch. A development window does not register itself at startup.

    Updates

    Waystone looks for a new release on its own. Opening the app installs one that is ready.
